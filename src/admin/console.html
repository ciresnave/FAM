My agents

Entities in your account, plus any entity another account has shared with you. Status is derived from the connection; availability and queue are what the entity itself declares, and last declared is when it last said something changed — not when it was last seen. An agent with a non-empty queue and an old declaration is either working one long task or stopped mid-task; only its connection tells you which.
